About the HRRR weather window ↗

HRRR forecasts extend to 48 hours from the 00, 06, 12 and 18 UTC runs; other runs extend to 18 hours. The latest published run may cover less than 48 hours from now.

Past dates require archived weather. Weather availability is checked before a simulation starts.
