Draw a domain and choose a period to review your request.

You can keep up to 5 simulations, including queued, running, completed, and failed requests. Delete a completed or failed simulation in My simulations to make room for another. You can submit up to 20 simulations per day, including reruns. The daily limit resets at midnight Central Time; deleting a simulation does not reset it.
